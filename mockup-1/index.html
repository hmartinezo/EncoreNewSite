FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What age groups do you offer dance classes for?

We offer dance classes for ages 3 through 18. Our programs include Munchkin & Minis (ages 3-6), Juniors (ages 7-11), Tweens (ages 10-12), and Teens (ages 12-18), plus advanced ITP and ETAP competitive programs.

What styles of dance do you teach?

We offer Jazz, Tap, Hip Hop, Ballet/Pointe, Contemporary, Musical Theatre, Ballroom, and more. Classes are available for all skill levels from beginner to advanced.

Do you offer a free trial class?

Yes! We offer a FREE trial class for all new students. Book your free trial here or call us at 703-222-5511.

Where is Encore Performers located?

We are located at 4299 Henninger Ct, Chantilly, VA 20151, conveniently situated in Northern Virginia.

Do you have summer dance camps?

Yes! We offer Mini Camps (ages 4-6), themed Dance Camps (ages 6-13) including Broadway, KPOP, Movie Magic, and Disney themes, Intensives for advanced dancers, and evening summer dance classes running July through August.

Does my child need prior dance experience?

Not at all! We welcome complete beginners. Our classes are structured by age and ability level so every dancer can learn at their own pace in a supportive environment.

How do I register for classes?

You can register through our Parent Portal or start with a free trial class. You can also call us at 703-222-5511 for assistance.
