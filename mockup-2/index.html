FAQ

Got Questions?

What age groups do you offer dance classes for?

We welcome dancers ages 3 through 18! Munchkin & Minis (3-6), Juniors (7-11), Tweens (10-12), Teens (12-18), plus advanced ITP and ETAP programs.

What styles of dance do you teach?

Jazz, Tap, Hip Hop, Ballet/Pointe, Contemporary, Musical Theatre, Ballroom, and more -- all skill levels welcome!

Do you offer a free trial class?

Yes! Book your FREE trial here or call 703-222-5511.

Where are you located?

4299 Henninger Ct, Chantilly, VA 20151 -- right in Northern Virginia!

Do you have summer camps?

Absolutely! Mini Camps (ages 4-6), themed Dance Camps (ages 6-13) with Broadway, KPOP, Disney, Movie Magic themes, plus Intensives and evening classes.

Does my child need dance experience?

Not at all! We welcome complete beginners and offer classes at every level so your dancer can learn at their own pace.

How do I register?

Through our Parent Portal, or start with a free trial class. Call 703-222-5511 anytime!
