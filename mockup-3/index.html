FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

What age groups do you offer classes for?

Ages 3 through 18. Munchkin & Minis (3-6), Juniors (7-11), Tweens (10-12), Teens (12-18), plus advanced ITP and ETAP programs.

What dance styles do you teach?

Jazz, Tap, Hip Hop, Ballet/Pointe, Contemporary, Musical Theatre, Ballroom, and more. All skill levels welcome.

Do you offer a free trial class?

Yes! Book your free trial here or call 703-222-5511.

Where are you located?

4299 Henninger Ct, Chantilly, VA 20151, in Northern Virginia.

Do you have summer camps?

Yes! Mini Camps (4-6), themed Dance Camps (6-13), Intensives for advanced dancers, and evening summer classes.

Does my child need experience?

Not at all. We welcome beginners and offer classes at every level.

How do I register?

Through our Parent Portal or start with a free trial. Call 703-222-5511 anytime.
